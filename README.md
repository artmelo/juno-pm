# 🪐 Juno — AI Product Management Co-Pilot

**Product School AI Product Management Project**

*Galactic Command for Product Managers*

## 🚀 Overview

**Juno** is an AI-powered Product Management Co-Pilot designed as part of my **Product School project**.

The project explores how agentic AI can support Product Managers in a resource-constrained environment, where prioritization, efficiency, and informed decision-making are critical.

Built around a fictional **RocketShip mission**, Juno acts as a virtual PM partner, helping transform rough product ideas into structured, actionable requirements while challenging assumptions and keeping recommendations aligned with business goals.

**The idea is simple:** Let AI handle the heavy lifting of product discovery and documentation, while the Product Manager remains accountable for decisions.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🚀 Idea to Requirements:** Transform a rough idea into a structured Product Requirements Document (PRD).
- **🛠️ User Stories:** Generate actionable user stories to support development planning.
- **🔭 Mission Radar:** Evaluate ideas against product goals, constraints, and mission priorities.
- **🪐 Context-Aware AI:** Juno understands the RocketShip project context, reducing the need to repeatedly explain requirements.
- **🧠 Critical Thinking:** Identify missing information, challenge assumptions, and highlight areas requiring clarification.
- **🎯 Human-in-the-Loop:** Keep the Product Manager in control of important decisions.
- **🌗 Galactic UI:** Space-inspired interface with light and dark themes.

## 🧠 Product Philosophy

Juno is designed around three principles:

**1. Context before generation**

AI should understand the product, its users, objectives, and constraints before proposing solutions.

**2. Autonomy with guardrails**

Juno can independently analyze, structure, and recommend, but consequential decisions require human review.

**3. Efficiency without sacrificing quality**

In a resource-constrained environment, Juno should deliver practical, actionable outputs with minimal unnecessary complexity.

## 🛸 How It Works

1. **Describe your idea:** Provide a short description of what you want to build.
2. **Add context (optional):** Explain the problem, target users, and desired outcomes.
3. **Generate requirements:** Juno analyzes the idea against its existing RocketShip knowledge.
4. **Review outputs:** Explore the PRD, User Stories, and Mission Radar.
5. **Make decisions:** The Product Manager reviews recommendations, resolves uncertainties, and determines next steps.

## 🧩 What This Project Explores

This project applies key concepts from the Product School AI Product Management curriculum:

- Prompt engineering and structured AI outputs
- Context engineering and knowledge management
- Agentic workflows and tool orchestration
- AI autonomy, confidence, and human oversight
- Guardrails, failure handling, and stop conditions
- Product prioritization and decision support
- AI-powered product discovery and requirements generation

## 🌌 Product Vision

**Build an AI co-pilot that thinks like a Product Manager, operates within clear boundaries, and helps a small team accomplish more with fewer resources.**

Juno is designed to extend their capabilities, accelerate execution, and improve the consistency of product decisions.
